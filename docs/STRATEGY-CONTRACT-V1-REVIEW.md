# Strategy Contract v1 - review decisions

The contract is structurally complete but intentionally remains `draft-awaiting-human-review`. Nothing in these playbooks is admitted as a training label yet.

## Recommended approvals

- Crustle's default identity is one resilient Crustle developed behind Mega Kangaskhan ex, with a conditional Kangaskhan pivot after the opponent commits its answers.
- Dragapult's default identity is a sustainable Dragapult ex attack chain whose damage placement follows a named prize route, supported by Energy and hand disruption.
- Prize-taking is conditional for both specialists. A knockout is not automatically preferred when it activates disruption, draw or a stronger response before the board is ready.
- Budew is a tempo decision rather than an automatic opening. It must buy more opposing tempo than the attachment, retreat and delayed attack it costs.
- More than one move may occupy a preferred or acceptable tier.
- Low-confidence positions abstain instead of receiving a mistake label.

## Corrections requested before approval

1. **Crustle list-version boundary.** The frozen v1 list does not contain Crushing Hammer, Psyduck, Cornerstone Ogerpon ex, Bianca's Devotion or Hand Trimmer. Earlier guide sections and some curriculum hypotheses mention those cards. The draft excludes those card-specific actions and retains only compatible strategic principles. Confirm that this is the intended boundary.
2. **Crustle mirror.** The guide's mirror discussion references Hand Trimmer, which is absent from v1. The draft reduces the plan to resource/deck-clock management, careful disruption and minimal board commitment. Correct any missing current-list mirror plan.
3. **Crustle into Dragapult.** The draft treats one protected Crustle as the default and a second as conditional because an underbuilt backup can become a trap or gust liability. Confirm the public conditions that should justify the second Crustle.
4. **Dragapult mirror opening.** The guide expresses a later preference for going second but describes important belief-dependent exceptions. The draft records “slightly prefer second,” not a hard rule. Confirm that wording.
5. **Dragapult into Crustle.** The draft delays Crushing Hammer until Crustle has meaningful attachment investment unless an immediate denial line exists. Confirm how much weight should be placed on early Hammer variance versus saving it for committed Energy.
6. **Terminology.** The tiers `preferred`, `acceptable`, `inaccuracy`, `mistake`, and `critical-error` are placeholders until calibrated win-probability thresholds exist. Confirm whether the UI should eventually call the final tier “blunder” instead.
